# 2. User Roles & Personas

- Requestor: Typically Shop Personnel who identify a deckplate issue, initiate the request, and submit the initial ESR.
- Engineer: Investigates the technical problem, develops a formal resolution and rationale and manages the ESR through technical sign-off.
- Engineering Supervisor: Technical authority responsible for reviewing the proposed resolution, references, and attributes, then providing final approval.
- Shop Personnel: Executes the physical repair or work outlined in the approved ESR and documents/signs off on the final completion status.
- Division Head- reviews the completed ESR for ESR closeout or disposition
